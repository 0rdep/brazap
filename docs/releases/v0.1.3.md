## New

- Record voice messages from a compact glass panel above the microphone button. Pause, resume, listen before sending, or discard a recording.
- Paste screenshots, copied images, photos, videos, audio files, and documents into chats. Review up to 20 attachments before sending.
- Add a separate caption to each photo, video, or document, including emojis from the built-in picker. Files selected from the attachment menu use the same preview.
- Send voice recordings and audio attachments through the shared local MCP service.

## Improvements

- Simplified pairing screen with a centered QR code and linking instructions. The last QR stays visible until its replacement is ready.
- Phone-number pairing now includes a searchable country picker, flags, calling codes, country-specific formatting, and validation.
- Consistent bundled color emojis in chat names, previews, headers, messages, and text fields.
- Voice and video calls now appear as call cards with their direction, outcome, and duration. Existing archived call messages are upgraded without changing their original data.
- Microphone recording continues through recoverable audio-buffer warnings instead of incorrectly reporting a permissions failure.
- English, Portuguese, and Spanish translations for the new flows.

## Downloads

Choose the binary for Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple Silicon, or macOS Intel. `checksums.sha256` contains the SHA-256 digest of each binary. Existing installations can find this release in Settings → Updates.

Captions are supported for photos, videos, and documents; WhatsApp audio attachments do not have a caption field. Release binaries are currently unsigned.
