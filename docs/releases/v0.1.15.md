## New

- Buttons in business messages now work. "Copy Pix code" and other copy buttons copy the code, phone buttons copy the number, call buttons start a WhatsApp call, and quick replies and list options send your answer the way WhatsApp does. Buttons that need WhatsApp itself, such as rating forms, tell you to open the message on your phone.
- Right-click a profile picture, in a chat's details or in a contact's profile, to copy or download it.
- Media previews are made from the photo or video when the sender didn't include one. For videos this uses the first frame, and for now only on Windows.

## Fixes and improvements

- The shared media, files and voice lists in chat details load the next page as you scroll instead of using a "Load more" button.
- The download buttons in those lists use a proper download icon instead of a chevron.
- Link previews that have no title, description or image are hidden.

## Downloads

Choose Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple Silicon, or macOS Intel. `checksums.sha256` contains the SHA-256 digest of each binary.
