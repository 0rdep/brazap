## New

- **Mention people in groups.** Type `@` in a group to pick a member from a list (↑/↓ and Enter or Tab, Esc to close); they are notified like on the phone. The list starts with **@all** (shown in your language, such as **@todos**), which tags everyone the way WhatsApp does. Mentions also work in photo, video and document captions. MCP clients can use the new `group_members` tool and the `mentions` and `mention_everyone` options of `send_message` and `send_attachment`.
- **Albums.** Photos and videos sent together share one bubble, as on the phone: two side by side, three as a pair over a wide one, and four or more as a grid whose last tile counts the rest. In the viewer, ← and → step through the album. Albums already in your history are grouped too.
- **Stickers with sound** show and play. The button on the sticker turns its sound on and off; the sound starts with the animation and both loop together. Opening one also plays it.
- **Stickers side by side.** Stickers someone sends one after another share a bubble, up to three per row.
- **Formatting as you type.** Bold, italic, strikethrough and code are styled in the message box, with their markers dimmed.

## Improvements

- The sidebar is more compact: the whole tile is clickable and the selected one is highlighted, the toggle sits next to a smaller logo, and the logo stays when the sidebar is collapsed. On short screens or large scaling, the sections scroll while Update and Settings stay at the bottom.
- The chat list shows short dates: the time today, then "Yesterday", the weekday within the last week, and a numeric date after that.
- More business chats show their verified name, including businesses that only reached this device through history sync.
- Messages WhatsApp only shows on your phone say so in your language, with a phone icon, in the chat and the chat list. Older-history notices are translated and clearer.
- Windows notifications follow the app language ("Photo", "Sticker", "New message"…).
- Attachment and chat menus widen to fit their text in every language.

## Downloads

Choose the installer for Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon, macOS Intel, or Linux x64 (`.deb` or AppImage). Standalone executables are still available for existing installations. `checksums.sha256` contains the SHA-256 digest of each download.

Installed copies update through **Restart and update** in the sidebar. Windows and Linux downloads are unsigned. macOS builds are not notarized yet, so the first launch needs **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (or `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Brazap.app`).
