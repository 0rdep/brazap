## Fixes and improvements

- Your phone is notified of new messages again while Brazap is open in the background. Brazap now reports this device as online only while its window is in use, as WhatsApp Web does, instead of the whole time it is connected. WhatsApp does not notify your phone while a linked device appears online.
- The "Announce online presence" setting now means "online while the app window is in use". Turn it off to never appear online.

## Downloads

Choose Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple Silicon, or macOS Intel. `checksums.sha256` contains the SHA-256 digest of each binary.

Install through **Restart and update** in the sidebar, or download the binary below. Release binaries are currently unsigned.
