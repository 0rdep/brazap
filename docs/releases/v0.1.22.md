## New

- **Installers for every platform.** Brazap now installs like any other desktop app, with its icon in the Start menu, Dock, Finder and applications menu.
  - **Windows:** `brazap-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` installs Brazap for your user, without administrator rights, and adds it to the Start menu. It can also add a desktop shortcut and start Brazap when it finishes. Uninstalling from Settings → Apps keeps your linked session and chat history.
  - **macOS:** `brazap-macos-aarch64.dmg` (Apple Silicon) and `brazap-macos-x86_64.dmg` (Intel) contain `Brazap.app`. Drag it to Applications. Double-clicking it no longer opens Terminal.
  - **Linux:** `brazap-linux-x86_64.deb` for Debian and Ubuntu (`sudo apt install ./brazap-linux-x86_64.deb`), or `brazap-linux-x86_64.AppImage` for other distributions.
- **Updates follow the installation.** The Windows installer copy, the macOS app and the AppImage keep updating through **Restart and update**. The macOS app is replaced as a whole. When Brazap is installed by the `.deb`, or anywhere Brazap cannot write, the sidebar shows **Download update** and opens the release page instead.
- **Voice message transcription.** Voice messages on screen and new ones you receive are transcribed on your computer with a local speech model, with automatic language detection. Audio never leaves your device. The model downloads the first time it is needed: Whisper base (85 MB, 99 languages) by default, or more accurate and larger models in **Settings → Privacy**, where you can also turn transcription off. MCP clients can use the new `transcribe_audio` tool.
- **Edit your messages.** Right-click a text message or a captioned photo, video or document you sent in the last 15 minutes and choose **Edit message**. The composer shows what you are editing; press Enter to save or Esc to cancel. Edits from your contacts and from your phone replace the text in Brazap and in search, marked **Edited**. MCP clients can use the new `edit_message` tool.
- **View once.** Turn on the **1** button next to **Send** in the attachment preview or the voice recorder to send photos, videos or a voice message as view once. Like WhatsApp, Brazap keeps no copy and shows a placeholder instead of the media. View-once messages you receive say to open them on your phone, since WhatsApp only shows them there. MCP clients can pass `view_once` to `send_attachment` and `send_voice`.

## Improvements

- The Status tab shows every status update as a conversation: author, content and time, grouped by day. Click one to open it in the viewer. Statuses no longer disappear after 24 hours; their photos, videos and audio follow the **Settings → Storage** retention instead.
- Deleted messages show when they were deleted.
- **Video calls.** The call view is larger and shows video in a portrait frame. Minimizing a call (the **–** button, or clicking outside it) now leaves a small picture-in-picture inside Brazap, so you can keep chatting during the call. The separate call window opens only while Brazap is minimized or closed to the tray, and it can be resized. A **Rotate video** button turns the other person's video when it arrives sideways or upside down.
- Calls in a conversation show their length in words ("31 min") beside the time, without a separate "Completed" label. The bubble's side shows who called.
- Fixed a crash when starting video on macOS.
- On Linux, Brazap's windows now match its menu entry and icon on Wayland desktops.
- With an AppImage, the MCP configuration in Settings → MCP points to the AppImage file rather than its temporary mount.

## Downloads

Choose the installer for Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon, macOS Intel, or Linux x64 (`.deb` or AppImage). Standalone executables are still available for existing installations. `checksums.sha256` contains the SHA-256 digest of each download.

Installed copies update through **Restart and update** in the sidebar. Windows and Linux downloads are unsigned. macOS builds are not notarized yet, so the first launch needs **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (or `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Brazap.app`).
