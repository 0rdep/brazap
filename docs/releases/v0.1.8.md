## Fixes and improvements

- Logging out returns to the start screen immediately while unlinking finishes in the background. Fresh QR codes are accepted after logout, including when a conversation was open.
- Drop photos, videos, audio, or documents onto the active chat to open the same attachment preview used by paste. Multiple files and additional drops into the preview share its existing caption, validation, and send flow.
- Conversations open with the latest 30 messages and load more while scrolling up. Message queries use the chat timeline index, load independently of sidebar refreshes, and show a loading or syncing state while waiting for history.
- Checking the active chat's unread count no longer rebuilds the full sidebar and all its message previews.
- Fixed a slow chat-list query that could leave Brazap on the startup screen with a timeout when opening a large message archive. Startup failures now show a clear error and a retry action.
- Improved QR pairing with a centered block of left-aligned instructions, a divider between linking methods, and a clear phone-number linking button.
- Simplified loading to a stationary Brazap logo and name. Six distinct messaging doodles are visible immediately and move faster, without routine status text shifting the layout.
- Fixed Windows startup and tray-restore flashes by waiting for the first presented frame and suppressing the native reveal animation during that handoff.
- Added the Brazap logo to the center of pairing QR codes while preserving the original payload, high error correction, and a clear border for scanning.
- The Brazap logo moves between startup, the QR code, and the sidebar as pairing completes, with a fade transition when reduced motion is enabled. QR dimensions are fixed from the first layout so the logo lands in the correct position without a later adjustment.
- The main app window now opens maximized.
- Moved the logo and app name above the sidebar. Collapsing keeps only the logo, and the expand/collapse control stays at the top. Sidebar width changes and labels now animate smoothly.
- Updates are checked shortly after startup and every five minutes, then downloaded and verified in the background. A **Restart and update** button appears above Settings when ready, with a matching compact control when the sidebar is collapsed.
- A verified, downloaded update also installs on the next full app launch. An app already running in the system tray is left running until you choose to restart or quit it.
