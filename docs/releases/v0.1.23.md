## New

- **A screen for each kind of conversation.** The sidebar rail now has **Chats**, **Groups**, **Communities**, **Channels** and **Broadcast lists**, next to **Calls** and **Status**. Every screen is always there, even when it is empty, and no list mixes kinds. A dot marks screens with unread chats. Opening a chat from search, a notification or a link takes you to its screen. **Settings** moved to the bottom of the rail; **Log out** is now the last item in Settings.
- **Communities.** The Communities screen lists each community's groups under its name, with its **Announcements** group first, shown with the community's picture, as on your phone. These groups no longer appear in Groups.
- **WhatsApp text formatting.** `*bold*`, `_italic_`, `~strikethrough~`, ` ```monospace``` `, `` `inline code` `` and `> quotes` now display formatted, with the markers hidden, in messages, captions and chat previews. Links and tagged people keep their text.
- **Search within a screen.** Searching in Groups finds groups and group messages, searching in Chats finds private chats, and so on. MCP clients can pass `kind` to `search` to do the same.

## Improvements

- Profile photos load again after logging out and linking the device again, without restarting. Photos that were hidden after a new pairing, while WhatsApp had not yet returned the permission to see them, are fetched again once.
- Business chats show the business's verified name (for example your bank or delivery app) instead of only a phone number.
- Messages a seller sends to a broadcast list now appear in your chat with that seller, under their name, instead of a separate "Unknown contact" chat. Earlier ones are merged into the same chat.
- Channels show their picture.
- Archived chats open from an **Archived** row with an icon and unread count, inside each screen.
- Saving a setting shows a short confirmation instead of text at the bottom of the page.
- Avatars without a photo no longer use punctuation as an initial, and names for unknown contacts, unnamed groups and broadcast lists follow the app language.

## Downloads

Choose the installer for Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon, macOS Intel, or Linux x64 (`.deb` or AppImage). Standalone executables are still available for existing installations. `checksums.sha256` contains the SHA-256 digest of each download.

Installed copies update through **Restart and update** in the sidebar. Windows and Linux downloads are unsigned. macOS builds are not notarized yet, so the first launch needs **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (or `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Brazap.app`).
