## Improvements

- Messages show in the chat the moment you send them. Stickers appear at once from the picker's copy. Photos, videos, files, audio files and voice messages appear as sending as soon as they are picked, while they upload, and the attachment panel and recorder close right away. Several attachments upload side by side but still go out in the order you sent them.
- Reactions show at once and no longer hold up the composer while they reach WhatsApp. If one fails, it is undone.
- Right-click a photo or video in the viewer for the same menu as in the chat: copy, download, add to My stickers, create a sticker, reply, forward and more.
- Brazap is ready sooner after it starts. Read markers your phone replays on every launch no longer rewrite anything that has not changed. Each of these updates looks up contacts and aliases once instead of several times, and the chat list no longer waits behind WhatsApp's own reads. On a large archive, the chat list could miss its 15-second deadline for about a minute after launch.

## Fixes

- On Windows, a video that had ended and was then rewound or restarted kept playing at a few frames per second. Seeking after the end now shows that frame and waits for Play. Frames are also paced more evenly around a seek, and a seek while paused shows its new frame.

## Downloads

Choose the installer for Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon, macOS Intel, or Linux x64 (`.deb` or AppImage). Standalone executables are still available for existing installations. `checksums.sha256` contains the SHA-256 digest of each download.

Installed copies update through **Restart and update** in the sidebar. Windows and Linux downloads are unsigned. macOS builds are not notarized yet, so the first launch needs **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (or `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Brazap.app`).
