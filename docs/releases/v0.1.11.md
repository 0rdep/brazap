## Features and improvements

- Links in messages are clickable and open in your default browser. Preview cards show titles, descriptions, and thumbnails included with WhatsApp messages.
- Added a dedicated **Status** tab. Status updates no longer appear as a WhatsApp conversation in Messages.
- View statuses in a story viewer with progress bars, automatic progression, pause/resume, previous/next controls, and keyboard navigation. Playback pauses while composing a reply or when the window loses focus.
- Unread statuses appear as a ring around the contact's chat avatar. Click the ring to open their updates. Viewed state persists across restarts, updates expire from browsing after 24 hours, and locked contacts remain hidden.
- Reply privately from the Status viewer with text or quick emoji replies. Replies quote the original status, and retrying a send preserves its message ID to prevent duplicates. Click a status quote in a conversation to reopen an available update.
- Audio messages automatically continue to the next audio message in the conversation, including messages beyond the currently loaded page.
