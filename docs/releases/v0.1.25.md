## New

- **Chat screens as chips.** All, Chats, Groups, Communities, Channels and Lists sit in a row of chips at the top of the chat list, each with its icon and a dot when it has unread chats. **All** lists every conversation together. When the chips don't fit, arrows show the hidden ones; you can also drag the row or turn the mouse wheel over it, and a chip you pick slides fully into view.
- **Search behind a button.** The search button beside the chips (or Ctrl+K) opens the search field above them; ← or Esc goes back to where you were. It searches every kind of chat and your messages at once, and the chips then filter the results. Chats whose names match come first (a name that starts with what you typed, then a word in it), ignoring accents, so "fam" finds "Família".
- **Stickers with sound from your videos.** Right-click a video and choose **Create sticker with sound**: the sound is cut to the animation's length and goes out with the sticker each time you send it. MCP clients can use `create_sticker` with `sound: true`.
- **Contact cards.** A shared contact shows its name, number and a **Business account** badge for businesses, with **Open chat** to write to them.
- **Pinned messages.** "Ana pinned a message" shows in the chat with the start of the pinned message; click it to go to that message.

## Improvements

- Sending a sticker no longer slows the app down: it shows in the chat at once while it uploads, and you can keep typing or send several in a row. Forwarding, opening a chat from a number or link, and other actions that wait on WhatsApp also run without holding up the rest of the app.
- Replies to a photo, video or sticker show a thumbnail of it.
- Groups that showed as "Unnamed group" get their name from WhatsApp.
- Calls, Status, Settings and **Restart and update** are buttons at the top of the sidebar, and the sidebar no longer collapses.
- The settings pages scroll on short windows or large display scaling, with Log out always visible. The settings icon is redrawn.
- Scrolling through a long chat no longer keeps running to its end or loading its whole history.
- Formatted messages no longer apply their bold or italic styles twice.
- Screen readers (Narrator, NVDA) can read the app, and reopening from the tray is faster.

## Downloads

Choose the installer for Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon, macOS Intel, or Linux x64 (`.deb` or AppImage). Standalone executables are still available for existing installations. `checksums.sha256` contains the SHA-256 digest of each download.

Installed copies update through **Restart and update** in the sidebar. Windows and Linux downloads are unsigned. macOS builds are not notarized yet, so the first launch needs **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (or `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Brazap.app`).
