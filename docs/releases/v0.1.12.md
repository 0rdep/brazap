## Features and improvements

- **Smaller database.** Message envelopes are stored without empty fields and without a second copy of each message body. This is lossless: the original WhatsApp message data is always kept, and new features can reprocess it. The one-time compaction runs in the background after startup, then the database file is rebuilt and kept trimmed. A large history typically shrinks by more than a gigabyte.
- **Media retention (Settings → Storage).** Choose how long downloaded media stays on this device after you last opened it, per type: photos, videos, stickers, audio and files (1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months, or never delete). Defaults: videos 1 week, photos 1 month, stickers, audio and files never. Messages are never removed, and removed media is downloaded again when you open it while WhatsApp or your phone still has it. Locked chats and unsent uploads are always kept.
- Videos now download when you play them instead of automatically when a chat opens.
- **Tags show names.** `@12345…` tags in messages now read `@Contact name` (or the phone number), and your own tag reads `@me` (`@eu` in Portuguese, `@yo` in Spanish). In groups, click a tag to open that person's profile.
- Captions now appear below images, videos, audio and files instead of above them.
- Right-click messages to copy text, images, or audio and video files.
- Right-click chats to pin, mute, archive, mark read/unread or delete, with changes synced to WhatsApp. Pinned and muted chats show indicators in the sidebar.
