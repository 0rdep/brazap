## New

- **Message any number.** Type or paste a phone number into the chat search, such as `(11) 98091-2212` or `+55 11 98091-2212`, or a WhatsApp link (`wa.me/…`, `api.whatsapp.com/send?…`, `whatsapp://send?…`), and choose **Message this number**. Numbers without a country code use your own account's country. Brazap checks with WhatsApp that the number has an account before opening the chat, and a link's message is placed in the composer as a draft.
- **WhatsApp links open in Brazap on Windows.** Brazap registers itself for `whatsapp://` links, so **Continue to chat** on `wa.me` pages opens the chat in Brazap. If another WhatsApp app is installed, choose Brazap in Settings → Apps → Default apps.
- **Create stickers.** Use **Create sticker** in the menu of a photo, video or GIF. Videos and GIFs become animated stickers of their first 8 seconds. New stickers are added to My stickers and sync to your phone.
- **Save stickers.** Add any sticker to My stickers, or remove it, from its menu or with the new button next to a sticker in the conversation.

## Improvements

- GIFs and animated stickers are now copied as GIF files, so they keep their animation when pasted, instead of becoming a still image.
- Videos sent without a preview now show their first frame on macOS and Linux as well as Windows.
- AI agents connected through MCP can open chats by phone number or link, make and save stickers, and save GIFs.

The first start after updating briefly rereads your video and sticker messages to find GIFs and saved stickers.

## Downloads

Choose Windows x64, Linux x64, macOS Apple Silicon, or macOS Intel. `checksums.sha256` contains the SHA-256 digest of each binary.

Install through **Restart and update** in the sidebar, or download the binary below. Release binaries are currently unsigned.
